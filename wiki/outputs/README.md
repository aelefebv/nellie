# outputs/

Standalone artifacts produced from wiki content. Things that are derived but worth keeping as files in their own right.

Examples:

- One-shot reports (REFLECT summaries, audit results)
- Generated diagrams or exports
- Snapshots intended for sharing outside the wiki

Unlike `inputs/`, these are write-as-needed. They're not the canonical record — articles are. Treat outputs as disposable: regenerate rather than hand-edit when the underlying article changes.
